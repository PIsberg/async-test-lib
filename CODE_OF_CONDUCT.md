# Code of Conduct

## The standard

Be civil and be useful. Disagree about code as much as you like; do not make it about the person.

Specifically, the following are not welcome in issues, pull requests, discussions or any other
project space: harassment of any kind, insults, personal or political attacks, sexualised language
or imagery, publishing someone's private information without permission, and sustained disruption of
discussion.

The following are welcome and expected: saying "I was wrong" when you were, asking what a change is
for before objecting to how it was done, and reviewing someone's work as you would want yours
reviewed.

## Scope

This applies in every project space, and in public spaces where someone is representing the project.

## Enforcement

Report a problem to **isberg.peter@gmail.com**. Reports are read by the maintainer and are not
shared further without the reporter's agreement, except where the law requires it.

The maintainer may respond with a private warning, a required apology, a temporary block, or a
permanent ban from the project's spaces, depending on severity and on whether the behaviour is
repeated. The maintainer will say which of those is happening and why. A complaint about that
decision goes to the same address.
